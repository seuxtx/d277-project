The Keystone State Pennsylvania

With a population of 13 million people, ranked just number 5 out of all states for population. Pennsylvania offers a vast selection of woodlands, farms, rural and suburban areas to venture out and experience. Attractions can vary depending on the section of the state that you venture out too, this can include coal mines, amusement parks, natural parks, and muesums. One of the original 13 colonies it would be introduced into the Union on December 12, 1787 and be home to one of the deadliests battles during the Civil War, The Battle of Gettysburg. The State capital of Pennsylvania is Harrisburg, which Philadelphia has more pople living there compared to the state capital.

Pennsylvania State Flower, Animal and Fish

The Ruffed Grouse is the state bird which is related to turkeys, guail, pheasants and ptarmigans. This bird mostly lives in the northern woodland regions of the state. Pennsylvania also has a State fish and flower, Brooke trout and Mountain-laurel.
