The Home of Bethlehem Steel

Bethlehem Steel was a steelmaking company that used all across the states, it saw massive production during both of the World Wars. The steel produced from the plant went on to contrsuct multiple bridges, warships, and skyscrapers. The Steelmaking giant would slowly loses steam as the years go on leading up to 2001 where it would offical declare bankruptcy and later dissolve in 2003. The original plant that is still up is now home to the Steelstacks which is an entertainment district in Bethlehem. Sands Bethwork would buy the property of Bethlehem Steel to build a casino which is present next to the Steelstacks.
